Why Production Stacks Degrade Silently

Latency Creep

Endpoints rarely fail cleanly. They gradually slow down from 40ms to 2800ms during peak load or database lockups. Traditional pingers only check for 200 OK, missing the degradation.

Silent Webhook Drops

Third-party webhook endpoints (Stripe, Twilio, Shopify) fail silently without alerting your team. By the time someone notices, hundreds of transactions have failed.

Enterprise Bloat

Datadog and Dynatrace cost $400+/mo and require complex agent daemons. Zentropy gives you immediate, high-signal telemetry with zero external bloat.
